## Historical scope

This batch established the initial Level 1 six-stage Reasoning curriculum structure for Quantitative and Verbal and introduced the reusable learning/activity architecture.

The implementation established:
- Level 1 Stages 1–6 curriculum structures;
- Explore/Extend curriculum halves;
- reusable Module/Lesson structure;
- reusable Activity/question architecture;
- separate Quantitative and Verbal progress;
- Reasoning separation from Foreign Languages;
- shared authentication for Reasoning routes.

Later remediation changed and expanded this implementation. The historical details are preserved in `docs/REASONING_REMEDIATION_HISTORY.md`.

## Current architectural rule

The accepted Level 1 · Stage 1 learner-facing experience is the universal blueprint for all Reasoning Stages and Levels. Current activity thresholds are **at least 5 Explore + at least 3 Extend + at least 8 Activities per track**, with exactly 10 delivered questions per Activity. Counts above the minimum remain Stage-specific.
