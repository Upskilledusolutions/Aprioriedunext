## 1. Curriculum purpose

Build a reading-led English-development programme for Indian school students. Reading should create the context for developing comprehension, vocabulary, grammatical accuracy and expression. The product should encourage independent reading and curiosity rather than becoming a conventional grammar workbook or examination-preparation course.

### Connected skill strands

1. **Reading comprehension:** explicit information, main idea, sequence, inference, cause/effect, character and motive, viewpoint, tone, theme, purpose, argument and evidence.
2. **Vocabulary:** meaning in context, word families, roots and affixes, synonyms and antonyms, shades of meaning, idioms, collocations, connotation and academic vocabulary.
3. **Grammar and usage:** sentence construction, agreement, tense, pronouns, articles, prepositions, punctuation, clauses, cohesion, sentence variety and editing.
4. **Written expression:** clear sentences, summaries, descriptions, narratives, explanations, reviews, literary responses, arguments and evidence-based responses.
5. **Listening and spoken expression:** reading aloud for meaning, retelling, discussion, oral explanation and presentation.
6. **Critical and information literacy:** fact versus opinion, evidence, source reliability, bias, persuasive language and comparison of accounts.

These strands should reinforce one another through meaningful texts. Vocabulary and grammar should be taught in context and then applied, not treated solely as isolated memorisation.

## 2. Explore and Extend reading principles

- **Explore fiction comprehension must use short stories, not novels or novel extracts.** Use stories of an appropriate length and difficulty. A short story that is unusually long or linguistically dense must be replaced by a suitable authentic published work of comparable syntactic and semantic complexity; do not write or commission an original adaptation.
- Explore should also include age-appropriate **non-fiction passages** so students read to learn about science, nature, history, biography, inventions, culture and Indian society.
- Each reading band below has at least 15 short-story candidates. This is a selection pool, not a requirement to use every story in every activity.
- **Extend provides a separate whole-novel pathway** with three candidate novels per band. Novel tasks may develop more demanding interpretation, evidence-based responses, comparison, vocabulary and written expression. Novel passages must not be presented as Explore short-story comprehension.
- The three novels per band are provisional candidates, not final adoption decisions. Validate length, lexical/syntactic complexity, themes, reading interest and suitability for Indian learners. Use an authentic published edition or translation only when its own rights status is verified; otherwise replace the work with a comparable eligible published text.
- Use a mix of Indian and world literature, humour, adventure, mystery, fantasy, moral dilemmas and varied perspectives. Review older works for dated language, stereotypes, colonial assumptions or sensitive content.
- Retention matters: useful vocabulary and grammar concepts should recur in later contexts, and reading engagement should be considered separately from test performance.

## 5. Public-domain and source-control policy

A public-domain underlying work is the intended basis for the reading library, but the rights status of the exact material must be checked before use. Under Section 22 of India's Copyright Act, 1957, the general term for a published literary work is the author's lifetime plus 60 years, counted from the beginning of the calendar year following the author's death; statutory exceptions and other rights may apply. See the [official Indian Copyright Office — Chapter V](https://copyright.gov.in/Copyright_Act_1957/chapter_v.html) and [IP India: Basics of Copyright](https://ipindia.gov.in/basics-of-copyright).

For every selected text, later content preparation must verify and record:

- Author, original title and publication details, as available.
- Exact source URL and edition used.
- Author death year and the basis for concluding that the underlying work may be used in India.
- Separate status of any translation, adaptation, annotations, illustrations and editorial additions.
- Any sensitive or dated content and the decision on use, adaptation or exclusion.
- Date of the rights check and the person or process that checked it.

Do not rely solely on a site's statement that a work is public domain in the United States. A modern translation, illustrated edition or adaptation may have separate protection even when the underlying story is old. For traditional Indian tales, verify the source and rights status of the actual wording used.

The owner has clarified that the learner-facing reading library must use **published literary and nonfiction texts by recognized authors**, in the public domain for use in India, or authentic published translations whose own rights status is verified for India. This is a binding content requirement, not a preference to be traded off against quantity or test coverage.

- Do not write or commission original stories, original fictional passages, or original nonfiction/informational reading passages for the learner reading library.
- Do not substitute an AI-written retelling, modernization, paraphrase or imitation of a famous author's style for the source text. The public-domain status of an underlying work does not automatically clear a modern translation or edition.

>
> **No original fiction or nonfiction reading passages may ever be used.** Every learner-facing fiction or nonfiction passage must be an authentic excerpt from a published work by a recognized literary writer/author, or an authentic published translation whose own rights status is verified for use in India. Do not create original passages, AI-written retellings, paraphrases, modernizations, or imitations of an author's style.
>
> **Rights failure means replacement, never original writing.** If a text, edition, excerpt, or translation is not confirmed eligible for use in India, replace it with another authentic published work by a recognized author that serves the same learning purpose and has comparable syntactic and semantic complexity appropriate to that level. Prefer a title from the owner's approved reading list. If no eligible approved alternative is available, stop and ask the owner to approve a replacement; never fill the slot with invented text.
>
> **Nonfiction candidate-pool record (2026-10-26).** The curriculum now records 75 nonfiction candidate works across five reading bands, with 15 distinct works per band and no work assigned to more than one band. This is a candidate pool, not an approved or rights-cleared passage set. **Only a work and the exact edition/translation confirmed to be in the public domain for use in India may be selected.** If any candidate, edition, or translation is not in the public domain in India or that status cannot be confirmed, replace it with another authentic published nonfiction work of similar syntactic and semantic complexity, appropriate to the same level and learning purpose. Do not write an original or AI-generated substitute.
>
> **Source approval is mandatory.** Use the owner's approved reading list as the source of truth. Record the original author, title, publication/edition, translator if applicable, exact source URL and excerpt location, India-specific rights basis, and owner/editorial approval. The underlying work's public-domain status does not automatically clear a modern translation, edition, annotations, or illustrations. Original questions and feedback may be authored about an eligible text; the reading passage itself may not be original.
>
> **Text-only replacement and regression protection.** For the current content-replacement work, change reading text and only the passage metadata strictly required to identify its authentic source. Preserve existing question-bank structure, IDs where valid, question counts, response formats, answer mappings, player/UI, navigation, analytics, authentication, and all previously completed build functionality. Do not undertake unrelated refactoring or unnecessary checks. The owner will review the content directly on the website. Once a stage's text replacement is accepted and that stage is ready to go live, publish that completed stage as the corresponding public live update; do not publish unreviewed content or bundle unrelated changes. No build, feature, or prior stage may regress.
